Chapter 1 – Aethelgard in the Clouds

Floating above the sea of mist, the city of Aethelgard glimmers like a lantern in endless night. Its winding streets are stitched together by bridges of brass and glass, and the soft hum of clockwork gears keeps time with the wind.

Chapter 2 – Lyra’s Discovery

Lyra, an eager apprentice, watches her grandfather coax a tiny, dim star from a cracked pocket watch. He does not merely mend the metal—he winds the fading light back into the heavens.

Chapter 3 – The Guiding Star Fades

When the city’s brightest guiding star dips into darkness, a chill settles over Aethelgard. The elders whisper of a prophecy: only the brave can climb the Brass Spire before first light.

Chapter 4 – The Ascent

Lyra straps her satchel, breathes the scented night‑air, and begins the ascent. Each step of the Brass Spire sings with ancient gears, echoing the rhythm of the city below.

Chapter 5 – The Star Restored

At the summit, Lyra finds the darkened star cradled in a brass cradle. She winds it with the ancient key her grandfather left, and golden light bursts, spilling across the clouds.
